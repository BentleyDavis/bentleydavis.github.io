Ownership at a glance. Suzanne Oliphant and Alexander Oliphant a/k/a Alexander Oliphant III each own an undivided one-half of whatever interest their mother Ione Oliphant held at her death (the judgment describes hers as an undivided 1/3, inherited from her husband Alexander Oliphant, Jr.). But Exhibit “A” itemizes her per-tract net interests, and those are mostly small — in practice each heir’s share runs about 0.4%–10% per tract, commonly around 1–3% (see the “Each heir’s share” column).

Chain of title

Alexander “Sandy” Oliphant, Sr. (d. November 7, 1946, intestate) → Final Decree, Hughes County Court, June 28, 1950: a sixth to each of four children, including Alexander Oliphant, Jr. → his widow Ione Oliphant (undivided 1/3 interest in the minerals below, by inheritance) → on her intestate death, vesting equally in her two children: Suzanne Oliphant and Alexander Oliphant III, each an undivided 1/2 of Ione’s interest. The 1992 court found plaintiffs in peaceable possession and confirmed their title. The per-tract fractions in Exhibit “A” derive from the 1950 distribution.

Whose share was sold? The 2020–2022 conveyances to Mekusukey Oil Company and Swadley Land and Minerals LLC were made by the Joe Oliphant Irrevocable Trust — the Alexander Oliphant III line of the family. A conveyance of an undivided mineral interest transfers only the grantor’s fractional share; it does not reduce the other heir’s interest. No conveyance by Suzanne Oliphant appears in the county index, so her position stands as the 1992 judgment left it: roughly 97 net mineral acres across the tracts above (subject to the lease terms noted).

How to read this table.
Undivided interest — a share of the whole mineral estate, with no claim to any particular acres; every acre is co-owned. It is not a contradiction: “1/5 undivided” simply means a one-fifth share, held undivided.
Net mineral acres (“undivided 2 acres”) — the acre-equivalent owned, spread across the tract — not 2 fenced-off acres. 2 net acres in an 80-acre tract = 2.5% of its minerals.
Fractions of fractions (“1/5 of 1/11”) — multiply them: 1/55 ≈ 1.82% of the minerals.
Each heir’s share — Suzanne Oliphant and Alexander Oliphant III each take one-half of Ione’s interest, so the last column is always half of the “What it means” figure.

Reading notes & caveats

  • The scanned type is faint in places. “Section 24-8-8” is read as Section 24, Township 8 North, Range 8 East; “Section 19-5N-?1E” is read as 5N-11E (the only Hughes-County-consistent reading; the first “1” is faint). “Section 7-9N-5E” is confirmed on close inspection as 7-9N-9E.
  • Evans Addition (Blocks 1–4, Holdenville) is drawn as the SE¼ SE¼ of Section 7-7N-9E, the 40-acre tract its metes-and-bounds description sits within; the town plat itself is smaller.
  • Per-tract percentages are net mineral acres (or the stated fractional interest) divided by the tract’s standard gross acreage, halved for each heir — approximate, since actual surveyed acres vary.
  • This documents ownership as quieted in 1992. Later conveyances, leases, or probate of either heir’s share are not reflected here.
  • This is a factual summary of a court record, not legal advice.
